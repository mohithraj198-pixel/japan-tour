HOW THE TOUR UNFOLDS

Our 10-Day Travel Program

01 A-01 Days 1–3

Osaka

Neon Dotonbori, street food culinary exploration & Osaka Castle.

Day 1: Arrival & Dotonbori

Airport transfer to hotel. Evening food walk through Dotonbori for Takoyaki, Okonomiyaki, and neon photo spots.

Day 2: Osaka Castle & Umeda

Explore historic Osaka Castle ramparts followed by sunset observation at the Umeda Sky Building.

Day 3: Nara Day Excursion

Short train trip to meet the sacred deer in Nara Park and visit the Great Buddha hall at Todai-ji.

02 B-02 Days 4–6

Kyoto

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Fushimi Inari & traditional tea ceremonies.

Kyoto • Fushimi Inari Path

Day 4: Ryokan & Gion

Check into a traditional Japanese Ryokan with onsen hot spring baths. Evening walking tour through the Gion district.

Day 5: Torii Gates & Bamboo

Early morning hike along the thousands of vermilion Torii gates at Fushimi Inari and the Arashiyama bamboo path.

Day 6: Golden Pavilion & Tea

Visit the Zen temple of Kinkaku-ji and participate in an authentic matcha tea ceremony.

03 C-03 Days 7–10

Tokyo

Shinjuku skylines, Shibuya Crossing, Tsukiji fish market & Harajuku culture.

Tokyo • Shibuya Night Rain

Day 7: Shinkansen & Ginza

High-speed bullet train from Kyoto to Tokyo. Afternoon in luxury Ginza boutiques and Michelin dining.

Day 8: Senso-ji & Akihabara

Morning incense rituals at Asakusa Senso-ji followed by futuristic neon explorations in Akihabara.

Day 9: Shibuya & Shinjuku

Cross world-famous Shibuya crossing, visit Meiji Shrine cedar groves, and view the city from Tokyo Tower.

Day 10: Farewell Tokyo

Tsukiji Outer Market breakfast, souvenir hunting, and private chauffeur transfer to Haneda/Narita.

04 D-04 Departure
